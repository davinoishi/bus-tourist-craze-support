Bus Tourist Craze

A bus-depot puzzle game for iPhone and iPad.

Support

Questions, bug reports, and stuck levels all go to the same place: emptyfolderprojects@gmail.com.

It helps to include your device model, your iOS version, and which leg of the journey you were on. If something looks wrong on screen, a screenshot is worth more than a description.

Common questions

Does the game need an internet connection?
No. Everything runs on your device, and the app makes no network requests at all. It works in aeroplane mode.

Where is my progress saved?
On your device only. Progress is not synced between devices, and deleting the app deletes it.

Is there an account, or any advertising?
No accounts, no ads, no purchases, and no tracking of any kind. See the privacy policy.

A level seems impossible.
Every level in the game is solvable — each one is checked by a solver before it ships. If you are certain you have found one that is not, please write in; that is a bug worth knowing about.
