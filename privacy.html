Privacy Policy

Bus Tourist Craze

No data is collected

Bus Tourist Craze does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. The app makes no network requests of any kind, so there is no server for your data to be sent to.

What the app stores, and where

The game saves your progress through the journey, your settings, and whether you have seen the opening briefing. All of it is stored locally on your device using the operating system's standard app storage, and none of it leaves the device.

When you tap the share button, the game writes a share card image into its own temporary storage so that iOS can hand it to the share sheet. That image stays on your device unless you choose to send it somewhere, in which case it goes only where you send it.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics or usage measurement.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking, and no data shared with any third party.
  • No accounts, sign-in, or email collection.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone access.
  • No purchases of any kind.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data whatsoever, it collects no data from children either.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it has stored. There is nothing held anywhere else, so there is nothing further to request or erase.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes what it stores or transmits, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date below will change.
